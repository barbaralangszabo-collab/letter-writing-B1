Letter writing B1

Core Objectives

This studio helps students practise writing letters and emails at B1 level. The aim is to build confidence with everyday vocabulary, simple linking words, and the right tone for formal and informal messages.
Operating Guide
  • Select Register: Choose between Formal or Informal modes to update the task list and phrase bank.
  • Choose Task: Pick a scenario from the dropdown to display the writing prompt.
  • Use Phrases: Click any phrase in the bank on the right; it will be added exactly where your cursor is.
  • Watch the Word Count: Keep an eye on the live word count (Target: 100–150) and the built-in timer.
  • Final Check: Complete the checklist before downloading your work as a .txt file.

B1 Key Points

Formal Letters
  • Say why you are writing in the first paragraph
  • Use a Formal style (avoid contractions)
  • Keep a polite, respectful tone
  • Organise ideas into short, clear paragraphs
  • Use a suitable ending (Yours sincerely/faithfully)
Informal Letters
  • Start in a friendly, natural way
  • Use an Informal style (contractions are fine)
  • Write as if you are speaking to a friend
  • Use everyday words and simple linking words
  • Finish with a warm, friendly closing
